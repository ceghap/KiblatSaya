```markdown
KiblatSaya is a lightweight, high-precision Qibla direction finder designed for Muslims worldwide. 

Built with the principles of speed and simplicity — just open the app, and the compass immediately points towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah with zero login walls, accounts, or friction!

🧭 1. INSTANT & ACCURATE QIBLA FINDER (ZERO LOGIN)

• Automatically detects your GPS coordinates with high-accuracy spherical trigonometry (great-circle forward azimuth) to the Kaaba.
• Live display of precise Qibla bearing degrees, cardinal directions, and exact distance in kilometers.

📳 2. TACTILE HAPTIC FEEDBACK & ROTATION GUIDANCE
• Dynamic alignment guidance that guides your rotation ("Turn X° to your right / left").
• Satisfying haptic feedback vibration and glowing emerald alignment ring when your device is directly facing the Kaaba (ALIGNED).

Customize your visual experience to suit your environment:
• Zamrud (Contemplative Emerald - Default)
• Nilam (Lapis Blue)
• Emas (Sacred Gold)
• Delima (Rose Ruby)
• Supports Obsidian Dark Mode (OLED battery saving) and Parchment Light Mode (high contrast) or follow your system settings.

📍 4. OFFLINE REGIONAL & GLOBAL CITIES
• No GPS signal or mobile data while traveling? No problem!
• Instant manual selector with preloaded coordinates for cities across Malaysia, Indonesia, Brunei, Singapore, Thailand, and worldwide holy destinations.

🔒 5. LIGHTWEIGHT & PRIVACY-FIRST
• Ultra-compact app size with instant launch speed.
• Your location data is strictly processed locally on your device solely to calculate the Qibla bearing. Zero location data is ever collected, tracked, or shared with third parties.
```
